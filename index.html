Scientific organizers

    Scientific rationale

    The aim of this IFPU focus week is to gather a small group of participants (~25) to discuss the most pressing open questions on dust and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) in galaxy evolution, from both the observational and the simulation perspectives.

    01

    ALMA and JWST for dust and PAHs

    What have ALMA and JWST taught us about dust and PAHs?

    02

    Dust & AGN

    What is the connection between dust and AGNs?

    03

    Are we ready for high-z?

    Are our simulations ready for the high-redshift Universe?

    Format

    The week is built around extensive, active group discussion, with the goal of shaping projects, proposals and papers around these topics and promoting new collaborations. Alongside talks from participants, the program includes dedicated activities:

    • Talks
    • Brainstorming sessions
    • Interactive discussions
    • Small-group work

        Local support

        There is no registration fee. Participants cover their own travel; IFPU provides full local support, including accommodation and daily meals.

        Priority for support is given to early-career researchers and to scientists based outside Italy.

        Accommodation

        Participants are hosted in local hotels or at the Adriatico Guesthouse ↗, on the ICTP campus by the sea, close to IFPU.

        Getting there

        • Trieste Airport (TRS): regional train to Trieste Centrale, or taxi.
        • Venice Marco Polo (VCE): shuttle to Venezia Mestre, then train to Trieste Centrale; direct coaches also run to Trieste.
        • From Trieste Centrale / city centre: bus line 6 towards Grignano / Miramare, or taxi.

        Please check current timetables before travelling.
